Privacy-Aware AI

AI That Respects Your Boundaries

Most AI tools send your data to servers you don't control. I build AI systems that run entirely on your hardware—your data never leaves your premises.

The Privacy-Aware AI Test

Does it still work if you unplug the internet?

If an AI tool requires constant cloud connection to function, your data is traveling to servers you don't control, processed by companies whose priorities aren't yours, and potentially used to train future AI models.

The AI systems I build pass this test. They run locally, using open-source models like Ollama that operate entirely on your own hardware.

Three Principles of Privacy-Aware AI

🏠

Local Sovereignty

AI that runs on your devices, not rented from a corporation. Your hardware, your control, your rules.

  • Models run on your Mac, PC, or local server
  • No monthly AI subscription fees
  • Works during internet outages
  • No usage limits or throttling
📉

Data Minimalism

Only collect what's necessary, keep what's useful, delete what's not. Your data has weight—treat it accordingly.

  • No unnecessary data harvesting
  • Clear data retention policies
  • You decide what the AI can access
  • Easy data deletion when needed
🔍

Transparent Operation

You always know what the AI is doing and where your data lives. No black boxes, no mystery processing.

  • Clear explanation of how each system works
  • You can see exactly what data is being used
  • No hidden telemetry or tracking
  • Open-source models you can inspect

Who Benefits from Privacy-Aware AI?

Small Businesses

Keep client data confidential. Process sensitive documents without sending them to third parties. Maintain competitive advantage with AI that doesn't leak your business intelligence.

Healthcare & Legal

HIPAA and attorney-client privilege require data protection. Local AI lets you leverage AI capabilities while maintaining required confidentiality.

Creative Professionals

Your work is your livelihood. Don't train someone else's AI on your writing, designs, or intellectual property. Keep your creative process private.

Privacy-Conscious Individuals

Not everyone wants their conversations, photos, and documents feeding corporate AI systems. Local AI gives you the benefits without the surveillance.
