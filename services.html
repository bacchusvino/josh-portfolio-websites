Digital Dignity

Technology Stewardship for Seniors & Families

You deserve to feel safe and capable with your technology. Not anxious. Not confused. Not afraid of making a mistake.

Digital Dignity is my approach to senior technology support—built on the belief that safety shouldn't require surveillance, and confidence comes from understanding, not dependency.

Safety Without Surveillance

Most "senior safety" solutions involve cameras, microphones, and constant monitoring. They protect the body at the cost of dignity and privacy.

I offer an alternative: mmWave radar fall detection that works through walls without capturing images or audio. No cameras. No microphones. Just presence detection that can alert family members if something seems wrong—while respecting the privacy of everyday life.

Scam Shields

Scammers target seniors because they're often trusting, polite, and less familiar with digital deception tactics. I build proactive protection:

  • Email filtering configured to catch phishing attempts
  • Phone protection that blocks known scam numbers
  • Browser security that warns before dangerous sites
  • Most importantly: training that helps you recognize manipulation tactics before you click or call back

You don't need to become paranoid. You need to recognize the signs.

Patient Technology Guidance

My NSCC training taught me that frustration with a student is always the instructor's problem to solve. I bring that same patience to every technology session:

  • Explanations in plain English, repeated as many times as needed
  • No judgment for "obvious" questions—there's no such thing
  • Teaching at your pace, not mine
  • Written notes and reference guides you can keep

Device Wellness & Organization

The digital clutter accumulates: photos never organized, email overflowing, devices running slow, passwords forgotten. I help restore order:

  • Photo library organization and backup
  • Email inbox management
  • Device optimization and cleanup
  • Password systems that are both secure and manageable

Local Resilience

Technology Stewardship for Small Businesses

Your business data is your business. Not Google's. Not Microsoft's. Not the latest AI startup that might not exist next year.

Local Resilience is my approach to small business technology—designed around data sovereignty, operational independence, and AI that actually respects your privacy.

Data Sovereignty Architecture

"The cloud" sounds convenient until you realize it means "someone else's computer in a location you don't control, governed by terms of service you didn't read."

I design systems that keep your sensitive data on premises:

  • Local file servers with automated backup
  • Self-hosted document management
  • Email systems you control (when appropriate)
  • Clear data maps showing exactly where every piece of business information lives

Local AI Workflows

AI can genuinely help your business—if it's implemented correctly. Most AI tools send your documents, conversations, and business intelligence to cloud servers where they're processed, stored, and potentially used to train future models.

I build Privacy-Aware AI systems using local models (Ollama) that run entirely on your hardware:

  • Local LLMs: Chat with AI that never phones home
  • RAG Systems: AI that knows your business documents without those documents leaving your network
  • Workflow Automation: AI-assisted processes that respect data boundaries

The test: Does it still work if you unplug the internet? If yes, your data stays private.

Mountain-Ready Infrastructure

Rural businesses face unique challenges: unreliable internet, power fluctuations, limited local support. I design for resilience:

  • Network architecture that continues functioning during internet outages
  • UPS and power protection appropriate to your setup
  • Failover systems that keep critical operations running
  • Remote access configured for when you need to work from elsewhere

Ongoing Stewardship

Technology doesn't maintain itself. Systems need updates, backups need verification, security needs attention. My stewardship model means you have ongoing support:

  • Monthly system health checks
  • Priority response when issues arise
  • Regular security reviews
  • Strategic technology planning as your business evolves
