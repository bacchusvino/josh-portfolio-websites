AI Philosophy

AI That Respects Your Boundaries

You've heard the hype. AI everywhere. AI in everything. AI that "learns" from your personal photos, emails, and conversations.

I offer something different: Privacy-Aware AI—systems designed with a simple test:

Does it still work if you unplug the internet?

If an AI tool requires constant cloud connection to function, your data is traveling to servers you don't control, processed by companies whose priorities aren't yours.

The AI systems I build for clients use local models running on your own hardware. Your documents, your conversations, your business intelligence—they never leave your premises.

Three Principles of Privacy-Aware AI

  • Local Sovereignty AI that runs on your devices, not rented from a corporation
  • Data Minimalism Only collect what's necessary, keep what's useful, delete what's not
  • Transparent Operation You always know what the AI is doing and where your data lives
